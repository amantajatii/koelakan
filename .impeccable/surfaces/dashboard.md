# Surface: Koelakan dashboard (all routes)

Scope: every dashboard route (overview, run your warung, rounds list/detail, agent chat, wholesalers, status). Mode: operate (owners complete tasks; overview also introduces the product to judges). Audience: warung owners on phones first, judges on laptops. Avoid: crowded screens, nostalgic/vintage look (user). The owner replaced the earlier emerald/pastel grocery world with an "Eduplex"-style dashboard reference: warm off-white canvas, white borderless cards, a navy floating sidebar, one lime accent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Koelakan as a calm, modern operations dashboard for warung owners: a navy desk with a lime pen. Navy carries structure and selection, lime marks the one current, done or pressable thing, and money is shown as large tabular figures and navy bars measured against PIHPS. It refuses the dark crypto dashboard and the kraft-paper ledger nostalgia.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas (#f4f4ef), white borderless cards at 20px radius with a barely-there shadow. Navy (#1f1f2e) for the floating sidebar, phone tab bar, dark focal cards, chart bars, progress rings and selected filter tabs; navy surfaces carry on-dark so focus turns lime. Lime (#d6f26b) only as a fill with navy ink; olive (#435a00) is the accent as text. Food pastels kept for staple tiles; lavender in progress, lime done, amber waiting. Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular numerals. Lucide stroke icons, produce cut-outs on pastel discs. No gradients, glow or radial washes. Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

STORY: The owner sees in seconds what needs them, how much they saved against the real market price, and what their agent is buying; limits and delegation read as safe and reversible. Judges grasp pooling, sealed bids and escrow from the same screens.

FIRST VIEWPORT (/, desktop): navy floating sidebar with the lime pill on Overview and the lime demo card at its foot; top bar with the testnet note, agent status and account pill. Main column: sentence-case h1 and muted lead, then the four staple cards two-up (pastel image disc, PIHPS / last price / below PIHPS), then the savings bar chart beside latest rounds. 22rem side column: the navy promo card (set up, see the demo), next-round countdown, round status rows. Phone: sidebar becomes a navy bottom tab bar, the promo card moves directly under the header, cards stack.

FORM: owner-chosen Eduplex-style dashboard reference, applied over the earlier grocery world (pastels kept for staples only). Signature moves: the savings chart (thin navy bars, newest bar lime with a navy tooltip) and the navy result card with the lime "% below PIHPS" pill. Earlier seed: 546b5c4bcbb4a366

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

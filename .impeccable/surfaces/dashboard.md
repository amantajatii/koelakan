# Surface: Koelakan dashboard (all routes)

Scope: every dashboard route (overview, run your warung, rounds list/detail, agent chat, wholesalers, status). Mode: operate (owners complete tasks; overview also introduces the product to judges). Audience: warung owners on phones first, judges on laptops. Avoid: crowded screens, nostalgic/vintage look (user). User pinned a reference: a fresh, modern consumer dashboard (white soft cards on a pale mint ground, one emerald primary, pastel category cards, icon side rail, real food imagery, shopping-list style checklist).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Koelakan as a fresh, modern grocery app for warung owners: soft white cards on a pale mint ground, one emerald action colour, a pastel tint per item, real produce imagery. It refuses both the dark crypto dashboard and the kraft-paper ledger nostalgia it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Ground pale mint with a soft corner wash; white cards, 20px radius, hairline green-grey border, soft offset shadow. Emerald primary only on actions, selection and the agent's live state. Item pastels: rice peach, sugar rose, eggs butter, oil sky, each with a darker same-hue text tint. One friendly geometric sans with tabular numerals. Icons from one stroke library. Produce photography-style cut-out illustrations for the four items.

STORY: The owner sees in seconds what needs them, how much they saved against the real market price, and what their agent is buying; limits and delegation read as safe and reversible. Judges grasp pooling, sealed bids and escrow from the same screens.

FIRST VIEWPORT (/warung, signed in, desktop): left icon rail with labels; top bar with greeting, agent-online pill, account chip. An emerald banner states this week's saving vs PIHPS with a produce illustration and the primary action. Below, four pastel item cards (image, price paid vs PIHPS struck through, qty ordered). Then a "Needs you" task list (confirm delivery) beside a standing-order checklist and next-round card. Phone: rail becomes a bottom tab bar, cards stack.

FORM: user-pinned reference fused with challenger digital-design-canon-warm-consumer-app-surface (chosen on the decision page); signature move: the savings strip on every item card (PIHPS struck, paid price in emerald, thin bar of % below PIHPS). seed:546b5c4bcbb4a366
